# On the Particular Quality of Tuesday Light

I don't have anything to say today, but I'm going to say it anyway, because that's sort of the point of this whole exercise.

The kettle took an unusually long time to boil this morning. I stood and watched it not boil, the way you're not supposed to, and thought about nothing in particular — mostly about the fact that I was thinking about nothing in particular. There's a certain kind of thought that only shows up when you're waiting for something small.

## A brief inventory of the studio at 9am

- One (1) mug, cold, containing tea I forgot about
- A pencil with no discernible point, in either sense
- Dust, doing what dust does, which is mostly just existing in the light from the window
- A half-formed idea about clay that I will probably lose by lunchtime

None of this is important. I'm not sure why I'm writing it down. Maybe that's the waffle talking — the sense that if you keep typing long enough, something will eventually arrive that's worth keeping, the way you sift a riverbed and mostly get gravel but every so often, something else.

*Or maybe it's just Tuesday.*

## Further nothing

I considered rearranging the shelf by the window. I did not rearrange the shelf by the window. This felt, at the time, like a meaningful decision, and now, several hours later, feels like neither a decision nor particularly meaningful.

There's a bird outside that has been making the same three-note sound for what feels like an unreasonable length of time. I don't know what kind of bird. I don't think I want to know — some things are nicer left as just "a bird outside."

Anyway. That's today's entry. More substantial thoughts to follow, presumably, at some point, from someone.
