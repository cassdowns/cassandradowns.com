## Relearning the Soil

In 2021 I dug a hole in my backyard. It was lockdown, I had nowhere else to go, and I'd noticed the soil wasn't very interested in growing vegetables - but maybe I could still make use of it.

I'd picked up the remnants of a home studio from a widow, his wife had passed 15 years prior and he finally felt ready to let go, so I inherited all the glaze ingredients, tools, materials, references, and supplies I could ever ask for - including a gas kiln and wheel - and set off trying to teach myself the art of pottery in the shed of my typical Australian post-war weatherboard rental. It was the kind of house that had been thrown up fast and cheap sometime in the fifties, on what used to be someone's cabbage patch a stone's throw from the old munitions works. Hence the inhospitable soil. My chickens routinely dug up chunks of lead and melted glass, which promptly discouraged our home grown veggie plan.
